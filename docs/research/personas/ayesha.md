# Ayesha, 20 · The Quick-Check Student

**Represents:** Students who check the portal briefly on a phone between classes
**Drawn from:** Interviews P1 and P2, and the smartphone-primary survey respondents (16 of 28)

## Context
Full-time 2nd or 3rd-year BS(CS) undergraduate. Uses an Android phone for short checks between
lectures, with occasional laptop use. Comfortable with everyday apps, but institutional terms and
grade-report codes are not always clear to her.

## Goals
- See at a glance whether her attendance is safe and how many more classes she can miss.
- Understand her weighted running total and the minimum score she still needs.
- Hear about at-risk attendance and newly published marks without polling the portal.

## Frustrations
- Works out attendance headroom in her head or from the percentage bar.
- Finds overall course standing and target final marks hard to calculate.
- Sometimes finds out about marks or deadlines late.
- Cannot always interpret grade-report codes and acronyms.

## Current workarounds
Mental arithmetic (13 of 28 survey respondents), guessing from the percentage bar (11 of 28), notes or
spreadsheets (4 of 28). Updates often arrive through classmates or WhatsApp first (19 of 28).

## What would help
Plain-language answers, a labelled status next to every colour, inline definitions of terms, an
optional Urdu/English toggle, readable high-contrast mobile layouts with large tap targets.

## Quote
> "I mostly open Flex between lectures to check if my attendance is still safe, and even when I see my
> quiz marks, I still have to work out how well I am doing overall."
