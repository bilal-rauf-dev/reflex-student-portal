# Anti-persona · Dr. Tariq Mehmood, Course Instructor and Academic Administrator

ReFlex is a student-facing portal. This is who it is **not** for.

## Who
A faculty or administrative user whose work is entering attendance and marks, configuring
assessments and managing academic records.

## Design decision
- No instructor or registrar data entry, and no bulk administration workflows.
- Missing student-facing data is stated plainly, naming who publishes it, instead of being hidden.
- Projections are labelled as estimates and link to the official read-only record.
