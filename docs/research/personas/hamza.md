# Hamza Shubman Gill, 22 · The Multi-Section Navigator

**Represents:** Upper-year students who make decisions across several portal sections
**Drawn from:** Interview P3, and the upper-year and multi-device survey respondents

## Context
Full-time 3rd or 4th-year BS(CS) undergraduate. Uses both laptop and phone, especially for
registration. Understands the academic workflow, but still needs consistent labels and short
summaries under deadline pressure.

## Goals
- Connect prerequisites, timetable, fees and registration in one place.
- Prepare a clash-free draft schedule before the registration window opens.
- Track fee deadlines and the status of his requests.

## Frustrations
- Moves between unlinked sections to make one decision (13 of 28 respondents report cross-section
  difficulty).
- Unclear registration-window messages.
- Duplicate fee screens and long payment instructions.
- Wide tables and downtime.

## What would help
Registration, timetable slots, prerequisite checks, clashes and any blocking fee status inside one
planner; a countdown while the window is closed; one chronological fee timeline; a cached-data time
stamp when the connection drops.

## Quote
> "The information is there somewhere in Flex, but jumping between Courses, Timetable, Registration,
> and Fee Challan just to make one registration decision takes way too much time."
