# Interview Guide

20 to 30 minutes, semi-structured. Follow the thread when something interesting appears; the order
below is a spine, not a script.

**Before you start:** consent form signed, participant labelled P1 / P2 / P3, no names in notes.

---

## Opening (2 min)

> "We are studying how students actually use the university portal. There are no wrong answers and we
> are not testing you. We are looking for the parts that are annoying. You can stop at any point."

- What year are you in, and roughly how many courses this semester?
- Last time you opened Flex, what were you trying to find out?

---

## Current behaviour (8 min)

*Goal: A1. Let them show, do not ask them to describe.*

- Walk me through the last time you checked your attendance. What did you do?
- **Do you keep track of it anywhere other than Flex?** *(If yes: can you show me? What is in it? How
  often do you update it? Has it ever been wrong?)*
- How do you find out when a fee is due, or when registration opens?
- Where do deadline reminders usually come from?
- Roughly how many times a week do you open the portal? What makes you open it?

**Probe, do not lead:** "Tell me more about that." "What happened next?" "Why that way?"

---

## Pain points (6 min)

*Goal: unprompted confirmation of the Phase 1 defects. Do not name them first.*

- Is there anything in the portal you have never understood?
- *(If they mention a code or abbreviation)* What do you think that means? Where did you learn it?
- Has the portal ever shown you a blank or empty page? What did you assume had happened?
- Has anything in it ever cost you something, a deadline, a penalty, a bad decision?

---

## Ranking (5 min)

*Goal: A2. Use cards, one event per card, and let them physically order them.*

Cards: attendance dropping · marks published · fee due · registration opening · grade change decision
· timetable change

- Sort these by how much they worry you. Highest at the top.
- Why is the top one at the top?
- Which of these do you find out about too late?

---

## Notifications (5 min)

*Goal: A3.*

- If the portal could message you, which of these would you want it to tell you about?
- Which would you switch off immediately?
- At what point does a phone notification stop being useful and start being annoying?
- Would knowing more, more often, make you feel better or worse?

---

## Close (2 min)

- If you could change one thing about the portal, what would it be?
- Anything you expected me to ask that I did not?

> "Thank you. Nothing here is attached to your name, and you can tell me to drop it at any point."

---

## Note-taking

Write what they **did** and what they **said**, in their words. Keep interpretation separate from
observation; use a right-hand column or a different colour. "P3 opens the portal 4 to 5 times a day"
is an observation. "P3 seems anxious" is an interpretation and belongs on the other side of the line.
