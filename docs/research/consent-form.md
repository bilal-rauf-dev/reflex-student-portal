# Participant Consent

**Study:** ReFlex, a redesigned university student portal
**Course:** CS3014 Applied Human Computer Interaction, FAST-NUCES
**Group:** Z-BASH

---

## What this is

We are students studying how people use the university's student portal, so that we can design a
better one for a course project. We would like to ask you about how you currently use it.

## What taking part involves

A conversation of about 20 to 30 minutes. We will ask how you check things like attendance, marks and
fees, and what you find confusing or annoying. There are no right answers and we are not testing you.

## What we record

- Written notes about what you say and do.
- Audio, **only if you tick the box below**. It is deleted once we have written it up.
- We do **not** record your name, roll number, email or any other identifying detail. You will appear
  in our notes as a participant number.
- If you show us something on your own device, we note what it contained, not who it belongs to. We do
  not photograph your screen without asking.

## Your rights

- Taking part is voluntary. Saying no has no consequence of any kind.
- You can skip any question without giving a reason.
- You can stop at any point, including after we finish, and we will not use anything you told us.
- Nothing you say reaches your instructors or affects your marks in any way.

## What happens to the results

Aggregated, anonymous findings go into our course report and may be published with the project. No
individual is identifiable in anything we produce.
